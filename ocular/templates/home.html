How it works

From upload to explanation in four steps

01

Upload

A colour fundus photograph in JPEG, PNG, TIFF, BMP or WebP. Rotation from phone EXIF data is corrected automatically.

≤ 10 MB
02

Preprocess

The black camera frame is trimmed so the retinal disc fills the input, then the image is resized and converted to the exact format used in training.

224 × 224 · BGR
03

Classify

Frozen VGG19 convolutional layers extract features; a small dense head trained on ODIR-5K turns them into eight probabilities.

softmax · 8 classes
04

Explain

Gradients of the winning class flow back to the last convolutional block to highlight the regions that drove the decision.

Grad-CAM

Under the hood

Built as a product, not just a notebook

Explainable by default

Every prediction ships with a Grad-CAM overlay and a before/after slider, plus the full probability distribution instead of a single label.

Input quality checks

Low resolution, under- or over-exposure and images that do not look like a fundus photo are flagged before anyone trusts the number.

Private history

Signed-in users keep a searchable record of past analyses. Anonymous uploads are processed in memory and never written to disk.

REST API

The same pipeline is available as JSON for scripts, notebooks or other services.
